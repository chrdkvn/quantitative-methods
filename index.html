What problems can this tool help solve?

Use linear programming when a decision must optimize a measurable objective under limited resources.

▦

Product mix and production planning

Choose how many units of two products to make to maximize profit or contribution while respecting labor, materials, or machine-hour limits.

Supported by this solver
⚖

Resource allocation

Allocate limited time, budget, staff, or raw materials between two activities to maximize output or minimize cost.

Supported by this solver
⌖

Blending and diet models

Find a least-cost combination of two ingredients or food sources while meeting minimum or maximum requirements.

Supported by this solver
Scope note

This version calculates two-decision-variable linear programming models. Other Quantitative Methods topics—such as transportation and assignment models, forecasting, inventory models, decision theory, and PERT/CPM—are broader subject areas, but their dedicated calculators are not included yet.
